Frequently asked questions

Is my data kept if I switch iPhones?
Yes, as long as iCloud is turned on. Everything is stored in your Apple Account.

How do I invite someone to help care for a pet?
In the Family tab, tap Invite next to the pet. The other person needs an iPhone signed in to iCloud.

Does the person I invite need to subscribe?
No. People you invite use the shared pets for free, as long as the owner's subscription is active.

How do I cancel my subscription?
In iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or in Patinha under Settings › Manage.

What happens if my subscription ends?
Nothing is lost. The app keeps showing your data in read-only mode until you subscribe again.

Can I send my pet's records to the vet?
Yes. On the pet's Health page you can create a report for the vet and a vaccination record, both as PDF files you can send by email or message.
