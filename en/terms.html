2. What Patinha does, and what it doesn't

Patinha records what you enter about your pets, such as vaccines, medications, vet visits, weight, symptoms, tasks and expenses, and reminds you on the dates and times you set. It can also create PDF reports from that information.

  • It is not veterinary advice. The vaccines, intervals and routines suggested for each species are only a starting point. Always check with your pet's veterinarian. The reports are notes made by the owner and are not a diagnosis.
  • It depends on what you enter. If a date or dose is entered incorrectly, the reminder will be wrong too.
  • Reminders depend on your iPhone. If notifications are turned off, or the device is out of battery or in a Focus mode, a reminder may not appear.
