Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · effective October 26, 2026

In short: Patinha has no server and does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account, and I have no access to any of it.

1. Who is responsible

Patinha is developed and maintained by Rodrigo Yamin Fraga, an individual developer based in Brazil. Privacy questions: rodrigoyamin@gmail.com.

2. What information the app stores

Only what you enter yourself:

  • pet details: name, species, color, photo (if you add one), birth date, breed, sex, microchip, spay/neuter status and notes;
  • health and routine: vaccines, dewormers, flea and tick treatments, medications, vet visits, weights, symptoms (including photos, if you add them) and tasks;
  • expenses for your pets;
  • the name you choose to appear as a caregiver, and a log of who checked off each task and when.

The app does not collect location, contacts, usage data, advertising identifiers or any information for tracking. There are no analytics tools, ads or third-party SDKs.

3. Where this information is kept

On your device and, if iCloud is turned on, in the private database of your Apple Account (CloudKit). This keeps your data in sync across your devices and safe if you switch iPhones. This storage is provided by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. I do not receive, see or store any of this data.

4. Sharing with other caregivers

A pet's data is only shared if you invite someone. The person you invite can see and edit that pet's information, including caregiver names and the log of who did each task. You can remove a participant or stop sharing at any time. When someone stops participating, that pet's data is removed from their device.

5. PDF reports

The report for the vet and the vaccination record are created on your iPhone. They are only sent to someone if you choose to share them, using the iPhone share sheet (for example, email or a messaging app).

6. Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. I do not receive your payment details: the app only asks Apple whether there is an active subscription. The start date of the free trial is stored in iCloud Keychain, in your Apple Account.

7. Notifications

Reminders for vaccines, medications and tasks are scheduled on the iPhone itself and do not go through any server.

8. Your rights

Because the information stays under your control, you can view, correct or delete everything directly in the app. Deleting a pet deletes all of its records. To also remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Patinha. If you have any request under the data protection laws that apply to you (such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), email the address above.

Patinha is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
